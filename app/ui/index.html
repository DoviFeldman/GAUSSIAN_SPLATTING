How to film a good video
  • Any phone works; 1080p is plenty (4K isn't needed).
  • Walk a full circle around the object, slowly, keeping it in frame. Get a few higher and lower angles if you can.
  • Avoid blur. Pausing for a second here and there is fine; blurry frames are skipped automatically.
  • Textured surroundings (wood, grass, rugs) help. Plain white tables, glass and mirror-shiny objects are hard.
  • Nothing should move in the scene: no people walking through, no pets.
